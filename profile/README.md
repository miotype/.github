### Speak, don't type.

**MioType** is voice typing and instant translation for the Mac. Press <kbd>Fn</kbd>, talk, press <kbd>Fn</kbd> again, and the words land at your cursor: in a terminal, your editor, a coding agent's prompt, or any other app.

- Mixed Chinese and English stays mixed. English terms are never translated
- Learns the words you correct, like `kubectl`, `useEffect` or a teammate's name
- Runs fully offline with a local Whisper model, or in the cloud with no API key needed
